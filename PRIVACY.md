# Privacy Policy for stopme

Last updated: June 11, 2026

## What this extension does

stopme shows a cartoon cat that reacts to how you spend time in your browser. To do that, it looks at which tab is active and how long you stay on it.

## What data it collects

The extension reads:

- The address (URL) of the tab you are currently viewing
- How long you spend on each site

## Where the data goes

All of it stays on your own computer. The extension saves this information using your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

- Nothing is sent to any server.
- Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party.
- There are no analytics, trackers, or ad networks.

## Removing your data

Clear the extension's stored data by removing the extension, or by using the reset option in its settings. Uninstalling deletes everything it saved.
